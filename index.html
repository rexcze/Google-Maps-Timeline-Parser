Your Data Stays Yours
  • Local Processing: Your JSON file is read directly by your browser using the File API. It is never uploaded to a server or stored in the cloud.
  • Zero Tracking: We do not use any analytics or tracking cookies that could see your location history.
  • Session-Only: Once you close this tab or refresh the page, the data is wiped from the browser's memory.
  • Non-Sensitive Settings: We use your browser’s localStorage to remember your UI preferences (like heatmap radius and theme) so you don’t have to reset them every time.
How do I get my data?
  1. Open Google Maps and tap your profile picture.
  2. Select Your Timeline, then tap the menu and choose Settings and privacy.
  3. Scroll down and select Export Timeline data as JSON.
